Common questions
How do I cancel a booking?
Open the booking in the Bookings tab and contact support with the booking ID. New bookings are limited to a 30-day window.
Payment or refund questions?
We use Stripe. A 20% platform fee is shown in the booking summary and on your Earnings screen (providers keep 80%).
Live tracking not working?
Make sure location is enabled for the app. Tracking is available on supported jobs within the 30-day booking window.
How do I become a provider or edit my showcase?
In Profile → "Become a Service Provider", then use "Edit My Showcase & Pricing" to add cover photos, work samples, and price estimates.
Report a problem with a pro (or customer)?
Email us with details and the booking ID. We review all reports.

Privacy & terms (short version)

Data we collect: name, contact info you provide, location (for matching & live tracking during jobs), and photos you or the provider upload for jobs.

Providers only see customer details for jobs they are assigned. We never sell your personal data.

Payments are handled by Stripe (we do not store full card details). Sparkefy charges a 20% platform fee on completed jobs (visible in the app).

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us. We aim to respond within 30 days.

By using the app you agree to our cancellation policy (contact support) and 30-day booking window for new jobs.

For the full privacy policy, data requests, or any questions, email sparkefysupport@gmail.com.
